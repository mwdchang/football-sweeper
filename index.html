Football Sweeper

Football Sweeper is a minesweeper game that takes place on the surface of a classical football.
  • The hexagon patches can contain mines, clear them to win the game.
  • The pentagon patches are neutral patches, they do not contain maines.
Use left-click to reveal a patch, use right-click (or long press) to flag a patch.
Use drag motion to move and zoom the camera.
